## What the device test must prove (beyond compilation)

1. A seat's pushed line is not talked over navigation or a phone call, and resumes after (audio focus).
2. With the OpenRun Pro 2, playback is A2DP and the phone records; headset-mic mode moves both to the Shokz.
3. A `speak` over `/api/ws` plays once during a call, is acknowledged with what was heard, and never plays after
   hang-up; with no call it is a notification.
4. The Board tile shows the floor holder and the watch pulses about 300 ms before the seat speaks.
5. The watch's mic button toggles the **phone's** microphone; the note screen reports "saved".
6. A shared link and a screenshot reach the ledger unchanged.
7. The voice panel's "Use headset mic" line moves both capture and playback to the Shokz (HFP), and names
   the output in use ("Use phone mic · Shokz OpenRun Pro 2"); "Use phone mic" returns to A2DP + Oppo mic.

## Uncertainty

- `speak_ack` is defined as a JSON-RPC notification `speak_ack`; C4a must accept that shape (documented in
  `server-push.md` §3b). The revision event name is `intent.revision` (or `worktable.intent.revision`).
- The owner-words route does not exist yet; the phone queues locally and retries on launch. Screenshots over
  1.4 MB go through `/api/files/upload-stream` and are referenced by path.
- The haptic onset is predicted (now + 1.4 s) because the chained TTS player does not report its first audible
  frame; the watch drops a cue that is already too late. The measured lead needs a real-watch run to tune.
- Pushed speech only plays while a chained call is open in the app process; a seat's line with no call, or with
  GPT-Live, becomes a notification. A line the gateway binds to a specific call id waits unless the phone knows
  that id; Kyllene does not send one yet, so C4a should omit `call` until the call id is carried in
  `voice_context` and echoed back.
- The output name is polled from the player when the call state changes, not pushed by the audio stack: it can
  lag a route change by one state tick.
- Upstream had no release newer than v0.17.0 (fork `master` is the upstream tip plus one CI commit); nothing was
  merged.

## Prior art

- Android audio focus: request `AUDIOFOCUS_GAIN_TRANSIENT` with `USAGE_VOICE_COMMUNICATION` and
  `CONTENT_TYPE_SPEECH`; the platform deliberately does not auto-duck speech and sends
  `LOSS_TRANSIENT_CAN_DUCK` so the app can pause instead (Android media docs, *Manage audio focus*).
  Element X applies the same rule to voice messages (`element-hq/element-x-android` PR 7454).
- Wear tiles: the Jetpack Tiles/protolayout model, with state cached locally rather than fetched in the tile
  request (Android *Tiles* guide) — the design `watch-plan.md` already chose.
- The 300 ms cue is a direct port of `agent-config/mobile/modules/worktable-audio`'s `HapticScheduler` (its
  drop-if-late law, measured-onset correction, evidence callback), with the Expo wrapper dropped.
